1. Theoretical Concepts & Operators

Operator Syntax & Wildcards Behavior & Evaluation
LIKE WHERE column LIKE 'pattern'
%: Multiple chars | _: Single char		 Performs substring and pattern matching. Case-insensitive in MySQL default collations.
BETWEEN WHERE column BETWEEN low AND high Evaluates inclusive numerical, temporal, or lexicographical intervals (>= low AND <= high).
IN WHERE column IN (val1, val2, ...) Evaluates set membership across literal lists or subquery results, replacing chained OR logic.
