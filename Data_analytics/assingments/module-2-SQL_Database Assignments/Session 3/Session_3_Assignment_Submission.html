1. Key Concepts

  • WHERE Clause: Filters rows based on a Boolean condition (evaluating to TRUE).
  • Comparison Operators: = (Equal), <> or != (Not Equal), >, <, >=, <=.
  • Logical Operators:
    • AND: All joined conditions must be TRUE.
    • OR: At least one joined condition must be TRUE.
    • NOT: Inverts the Boolean evaluation of a condition.
  • Precedence: Parentheses () > NOT > AND > OR.

2. Demo Execution: Employees Filter (Salary > 50000 AND IT Dept)
