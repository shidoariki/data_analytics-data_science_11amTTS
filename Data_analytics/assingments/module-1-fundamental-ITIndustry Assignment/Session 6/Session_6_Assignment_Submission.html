Task 1 Flipkart E-Commerce: 3 Core Business Questions

Question 1: Cash-on-Delivery (COD) vs. Prepaid Return-to-Origin (RTO) Disparity

"What is the exact correlation between payment method (COD vs. UPI/Card) and doorstep return rates across product categories, and how much margin is lost due to unfulfilled COD deliveries in Tier-2/3 cities?"

Question 2: Discount Elasticity vs. Return Propensity in Fashion

"Does heavy discounting (> 40%) in Fashion attract 'serial returners' who order multiple sizes with the intent to return, and what is the net profit per customer after accounting for reverse logistics costs?"

Question 3: Customer Satisfaction & Rating Threshold as a Churn Predictor

"At what customer review rating threshold (e.g. < 3.2 stars) or delivery delay threshold (> 48 hours) does a customer's 90-day repeat purchase probability drop below 15%, and which automated intervention best mitigates churn?"

Task 2 & 3 Expected Outcomes & Executive Decision-Making

Reference Dataset: Flipkart_Orders_Returns_Dataset.csv / .xlsx (250 records)

Expected Outcome Analytical Deliverable Business Decision Impact
Automated RTO Risk Scoring Machine learning classification scoring incoming COD orders as Low, Medium, or High Risk. Mandate a nominal Rs. 49 advance shipping deposit for High-Risk orders, cutting reverse logistics losses by Rs. 18.5M quarterly.
Catalog Return Diagnostics Category-level matrix isolating SKUs with > 25% returns due to 'Size/Fit Issues'. Replace static 2D size charts with 3D interactive size recommendation widgets, lifting retained apparel sales by 12%.
Cohort Retention Curve Survival curves tracking 30/60/90-day repeat purchases segmented by first-purchase category. Reallocate ad budgets toward high-LTV categories (Mobiles and Electronics) over high-churn promotional fashion.

Task 4 Real-World Scenario: Swiggy Operations Analyst

Target Business Question:
"How can Swiggy reduce delivery partner idle wait times at restaurant kitchens in Bangalore from 11.4 minutes to under 4.0 minutes during peak lunch hours, without compromising food freshness or missing the 30-minute delivery SLA?"

Required Dataset Telemetry:

Data Field Source Purpose
order_id, order_timestampOrder ServiceBaseline timeline kickoff.
restaurant_id, cuisine_typeMerchant DBSegments fast prep (burgers: 7m) vs. slow prep (biryani: 22m).
kitchen_prep_start_timeKitchen DisplayCaptures actual cooking initiation.
rider_arrival_timestampRider GPS GeofencePins the exact arrival time within 100m.
rider_handover_timestampQR Scan at CounterCalculates exact partner idle wait duration.
Expected Outcome & Measurable Impact:
By transitioning from static dispatch to a Dynamic Just-In-Time (JIT) Predictive Dispatch Model—which factors in live kitchen velocity and only dispatches riders when food is within 4 minutes of completion—idle wait time drops from 11.4 to 3.8 minutes (66% reduction). Delivery partners complete 1.8 additional orders per day, expanding platform capacity by 19% and saving Rs. 7.2M monthly in fleet delay compensation.
