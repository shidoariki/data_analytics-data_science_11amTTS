Task 3 Bivariate Scatter Plot: Balls Faced vs. Runs Scored

Figure 3.0: Scatter plot illustrating linear association with ordinary least squares (OLS) regression fit.
Regression Equation: Runs Scored ≈ 1.48 × (Balls Faced) - 3.2
Pearson Correlation (r): +0.923 (Very Strong Positive)
Goodness of Fit (R²): 0.852 (85.2% of run variance explained by balls faced)

Key Takeaway: As players occupy the crease for longer durations, run accumulation increases at a near-linear rate of ~1.48 runs per ball, showing how time at the crease is the strongest predictor of aggregate score.
